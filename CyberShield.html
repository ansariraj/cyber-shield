ABOUT CYBER AWARENESS

What is Cyber Awareness?

Cyber awareness means knowing how to stay safe while using the internet and digital devices. It helps us understand common threats like phishing, online scams, fake links, malware, and password theft.

It also teaches us how to protect our personal information, accounts, and money online. Through our Cyber Shield project, we aim to spread awareness and encourage people to use the internet safely and responsibly.

STAY ALERT

Common Cyber Threats

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails, messages and websites designed to steal passwords or personal information.

🦠

Malware

Malicious software that can damage devices or steal sensitive information.

💳

Online Fraud

Fake offers, payment requests and scams designed to trick people into losing money.

👤

Identity Theft

Stealing personal information and using it without the owner's permission.

PROTECT YOURSELF

Cyber Safety Tips

01

Use Strong Passwords

Create long and unique passwords for different accounts.

02

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Use 2FA whenever it is available to add another layer of account protection.

03

Never Share OTPs

Banks and legitimate services do not require you to share your OTP with strangers.

04

Verify Suspicious Links

Check links and websites carefully before entering your personal information.

05

Keep Devices Updated

Install security and software updates regularly.

06

Protect Personal Information

Avoid sharing passwords, PINs and sensitive information online.
